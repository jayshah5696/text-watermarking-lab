## Inconvenient evidence retained

- The three short Stage 5 marked smoke rows stayed below strict `z > 3`.
- Rank `1001` had identical marked and control IDs through 80 copied tokens.
- Four of 1,000 natural-web rows crossed under all-pair counting.
- The maximum natural-web row moved below the cutoff under distinct-pair counting.
- Some Stage 7 row differences overlapped or moved against the mean.
- No Stage 7 pair supported 400 copied tokens in both generated conditions.
- Two paraphrase reviews remain uncertain.
- Ranks 1004 and 1006 had lower z at delta 3 than at delta 2.
