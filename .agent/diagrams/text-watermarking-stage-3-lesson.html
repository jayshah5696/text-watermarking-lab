The complete story without animation

The recorded score increase run selects 20 token pieces. Twelve selected pieces receive 2 and eight receive 0. Jack receives 2 at position 1. Its chance rises from 11.6422% to 18.5816%, but both runs still choose Jack. At position 2, the control chooses paused and the score increase run chooses climbed. For the score increase continuation, positions 2 to 20 contain 11 green tokens among 19 checked positions. The full result is 21/39 with z 4.1603.

Show how the program calculates Jack's chance

The program adds 2, divides every adjusted score by temperature 0.8, applies top-p 0.95, keeps at most 40 candidates with top-k, and converts the remaining scores into chances that add to 100%.

Before filtering, the relative green odds multiplier is exp(2 / 0.8) = 12.1825. This is a calculation from the fixed settings, not a quality measurement. Being green does not force a token. A token can receive the score increase and still be removed by a later filter.

04

Stage 3 measured token chances, not writing quality.

The key does not evaluate meaning. Raising green scores can reduce the chance of an unchanged candidate.

Measured here

The chance for He falls from 25.4290% to 3.3315%.

This proves that the first-position sampling chances changed. Stage 3 did not measure whether the change helped or harmed the sentence.

Not measured here

No quality test was run.

The lab collected no blind ratings or task scores. Reading one pair of short continuations cannot tell us whether writing quality rose, fell, or stayed the same.

No row proves AI origin or authorship. The checker compares copied text with this lab profile and key. It does not identify a private vendor watermark.

Outside comparison: SynthID-Text

This lab uses its own KGW-style teaching rule. It does not reproduce the upstream KGW implementation, SynthID-Text, or Claude's private method.

Dathathri et al. describe a different method called Tournament sampling. Their non-distortion statement averages over watermark randomness and depends on the tested configuration. The paper reports no statistically significant difference in user feedback across about 20 million Gemini responses. That result belongs to SynthID and its Gemini test. It is not a quality result for Stage 3.

Related primary sources: KGW, Three Bricks, and Robust Distortion-free Watermarks.
