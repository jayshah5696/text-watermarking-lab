Build the watermarked score

At a 25 percent baseline, 26 checks average 6.5 green hits across repetitions. This saved text has 11.

z = (11 - 6.5) / sqrt(26 x .25 x .75) = 2.0381

The result sits 2.0381 usual null movements above the configured average. It is insufficient evidence at this cutoff. It does not prove human origin or absence of a watermark.
