09 / Read the result narrowly

Two hits in four steps explain the mechanism.

They do not establish a detection rate. Stage 2 defines no decision threshold and produces no positive or negative detector verdict.

No language model

The scores and labels are fixed teaching inputs.

No accuracy result

Four positions cannot measure false alarms or detection power.

No production security

The published key and toy hash rule provide no secrecy or attribution.

No generic AI detector

This checker recognizes only the configured toy rule and key.

Show formal names and the toy selector

The selected share is called gamma. Here it is 0.25, or five of 20 options. The score boost is called delta. Here it is 2.0.

For one boosted token compared with one unchanged token, adding 2 multiplies their probability ratio by exp(2), about 7.389. It does not multiply the boosted token's final probability by 7.389 because all 20 probabilities are recalculated together.
